For estate agencies and property professionals | Genoa and Liguria

Give international clients a reliable next step after the sale.

Lianor works as an independent coordination partner for foreign owners who need help with property setup, renovation follow-up, or ongoing local tasks. The agency or professional keeps the client relationship and responsibility for their own work.

Where Lianor helps

The sale or technical appointment ends. The practical questions often do not.

The client may still need multilingual explanations, written summaries, utility appointments, access for suppliers, deliveries, and someone who can explain what happens next.

Instead of leaving those requests with the agency or professional as unpaid follow-up, Lianor turns them into a separate service with its own proposal, scope, and updates.

A straightforward referral model

Introduce Lianor only when the client needs it; everyone keeps their own role.

Spot the need

The agency or professional identifies a client who needs multilingual communication or practical local follow-up.

Introduce Lianor

Lianor is introduced as an independent service, separate from the sale or technical appointment.

We scope and quote

Lianor speaks with the client, identifies the practical need, and sends a separate proposal.

Work stays separate

The client appoints the agency, professionals, contractors, and Lianor separately for their respective work.

What the partner gains

Better service for the client, less unpaid follow-up for the professional.

The model is designed to make the client journey easier without creating an undefined assistant role inside the agency or professional practice.

Clear multilingual communication

Clear summaries and organized questions help the client understand what is happening and make decisions on time.

Fewer post-sale requests

Utilities, deliveries, access, and practical requests move into a separate paid service.

A stronger handover

The client has a local point of coordination after completion, while the original professional relationship remains intact.

Clear roles

Lianor coordinates the practical work without entering the sale or replacing professional judgment.

The estate agent retains responsibility for the transaction. Architects, surveyors, engineers, lawyers, notaries, accountants, and contractors remain responsible for their professional work.

Lianor coordinates multilingual client communication, practical post-purchase tasks, owner updates, and agreed local follow-up. The client contracts with Lianor separately, and no fee depends on a property sale.
