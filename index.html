Work, study and new goals

Choose your Cambridge level

Whether you want to progress in your career, study or achieve a personal goal, we start with your English and the qualification you want to earn.

B2 First

Certify independent English

For a new role, a stronger résumé or a study experience abroad. Develop the ability to communicate clearly, understand texts and conversations, and handle the B2 exam tasks.

C1 Advanced

Make more of your skills

For using English at work, working toward a promotion or pursuing a degree in English. Develop the precision and flexibility to discuss ideas, write detailed texts and prepare for the C1 exam.

C2 Proficiency

Take English to a high level

For working in English, managing complex communication or certifying an advanced command of the language. Refine nuance, register and accuracy through focused preparation for the C2 exam.
