Why owners need a local coordinator

A renovation abroad is difficult when every person holds a different piece of the picture.

The architect, building manager, contractor, installers, suppliers, and delivery teams may all affect the same schedule, but their updates often arrive separately.

Lianor brings those updates together, follows up locally, and gives the owner a clear view of decisions, delays, and next steps.

What Lianor coordinates

Dedicated owner-side coordination from setup to handover.

Each assignment is tailored to the home, the owner’s priorities, and the appointed technical team. Lianor accepts a limited number of active renovation assignments so each project receives consistent attention, timely follow-up, and clear updates. When useful, the work can also involve a carefully selected network of trusted local professionals, suppliers, and artisans.

Working with the appointed technical team

Lianor keeps the project connected; the appointed professionals remain responsible for technical decisions.

The architect, surveyor, engineer, contractor, lawyer, accountant, and other appointed specialists remain responsible for design, compliance, technical quality, safety, and regulated advice.

Lianor coordinates communication, access, decisions, and owner updates around that professional work. Every engagement identifies the technical lead, included visits, reporting rhythm, and decision process.
