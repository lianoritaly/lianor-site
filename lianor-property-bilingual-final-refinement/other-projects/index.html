Why it matters

A project becomes more expensive when decisions, responsibilities, and updates are scattered.

International events and projects can involve venues, suppliers, technical production, guests, transportation, documents, and local participants. From abroad, even a small uncertainty can delay the work or create avoidable costs.

Lianor keeps the practical steps connected, follows up on agreed actions locally, and gives the client a clear view of what is complete, what is still open, and what needs a decision.
