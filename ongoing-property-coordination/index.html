A local point of contact for your home in Liguria, even when you are away.

For homes used only part of the year

Small tasks are easier to manage before they become urgent.

A second home still needs local attention when you are away. A technician may need access, routine maintenance must be arranged, or the home may need to be opened and prepared before your arrival.

Instead of handling each issue from abroad as it appears, you agree on a regular service and receive clear updates in one place.

What the service can include

The practical tasks that keep an occasional home ready.

Choose a regular rhythm that fits the home, with extra visits or supplier appointments quoted separately.

Scheduled property checks

Scheduled visits with photos and a short note on the home’s visible condition and anything that needs attention.

Access for maintenance

Arrange routine appointments and provide agreed access for technicians, cleaners, or suppliers.

Before you arrive

Open, prepare, or close the home using a checklist agreed for the property.

One clear record

Keep contacts, appointments, completed tasks, and outstanding maintenance together.

A clear monthly or seasonal agreement

You know what is included, when updates arrive, and what counts as an extra visit.

The agreement sets the included visits, update schedule, contact hours, geographic area, and response to unexpected requests.

If a visit reveals a technical issue, Lianor records it and helps you contact the appropriate professional.

Ongoing coordination inquiry

Tell us how often you use the home and what usually needs local attention.

We will assess whether a monthly or seasonal arrangement is practical for the location and the level of service you need.
