Projects and outcomes across the network

IELTS 7.5+ and UK university offers

Through a collaboration with an education partner in China, more than 20 Chinese students achieved IELTS scores of at least 7.5 and received offers from their target UK universities for the 2026/27 academic year.

An annual scholarship program run by a network partner provided two students with fully funded SAT preparation and university application support. Their applications covered the UK, the US and China; the two recipients are now studying at the London School of Economics and Duke Kunshan University.

An essay competition focused on studying abroad, organized with a partner school in Poland, offered free SAT preparation and university application guidance to students who submitted the strongest essays.
