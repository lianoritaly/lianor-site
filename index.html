Your English,
ready for the exam

Certify the English you need to study abroad, take part in Erasmus and apply for undergraduate degrees, master’s programmes and PhDs. Prepare for B2 First, C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency with 30- or 60-hour online courses shaped around your level and your goal.

Your teacher

Over ten years of experience teaching Cambridge exam preparation courses. Giulia is a Cambridge English speaking examiner, holds the Cambridge DELTA and is currently studying for a master’s in English Language Assessment at the University of Cambridge.

Experience that connects classroom teaching with an understanding of how exam performance is assessed.

A qualification with a purpose

Take your English further

Start with what you want to do, so your time and study go towards a qualification that serves your plans.

B2 First

Turn plans into possibilities

Certify your English at B2 for an Erasmus exchange and your next step at university. Prepare through exam tasks, mock exams and feedback, so you approach the exam with a clear method and greater confidence.

C1 Advanced

Study and work in English

For an English-taught degree, a master’s abroad or a PhD: demonstrate advanced English and prepare to study in an international setting. Work on complex texts, developing arguments and clear writing alongside your exam preparation.

C2 Proficiency

Use English with greater mastery

For research, advanced academic study or an international career: certify your command of English at C2. Refine precision, nuance and register to express complex ideas effectively and prepare for the exam.

Ask about this course

Each course starts with a minimum of 5 participants. Before enrolment, we check your starting level and agree the schedule and lesson times.

The fee includes online lessons, course activities and mock exam practice, and feedback on assigned work. The Cambridge exam fee and any coursebooks are not included; any materials to purchase are specified before enrolment.
