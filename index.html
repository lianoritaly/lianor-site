Why owners choose local coordination

Being abroad should not mean being in the dark.

A home in Liguria can involve building managers, utility companies, technicians, contractors, suppliers, and delivery teams. When updates are scattered across calls and messages, small delays and unanswered questions can quickly become expensive or stressful.

Lianor keeps the practical work moving and gives you one clear picture of what has happened, what is delayed, and what you need to decide. Our proactive approach ensures that issues are fixed before they become problems.
