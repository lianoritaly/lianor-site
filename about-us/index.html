About Us

Lianor brings together experience in English language teaching, language assessment and international university admissions support.

Giulia, founder of Lianor

Giulia lived and studied in the UK for several years, completing an MA (Hons) and an MSc, and is currently taking a course of study in English Language Assessment at the University of Cambridge.

She holds the Cambridge DELTA, an advanced diploma in English language teaching, and has several years of experience as a Cambridge English Speaking Examiner across levels A2-C2.

Academic expertise and personal attention

International experience

The team supports students with language learning, exams and university applications in the UK, the US, Europe and China. Most of our teachers, tutors and mentors have direct experience of leading universities.

A tutor network across countries

Our network brings together teachers, tutors and mentors with experience across Europe, the Middle East, Asia and North America, including graduates of Cambridge and Oxford.

Teaching and mentoring

The network includes professionals with school and university teaching experience, PhD holders from prestigious universities, and IELTS and Cambridge English speaking examiners.

International collaborations

Our network includes collaborations with schools and education providers in Poland and China, bringing together complementary expertise.

Poland

A collaboration with a high school led to an essay competition for students interested in studying abroad, with free SAT preparation and university application guidance for those who submitted the strongest essays.

China

Our collaborations with education providers specializing in IELTS preparation, international applications and undergraduate admissions in China also support international students interested in studying in the country with no prior knowledge of Chinese.
