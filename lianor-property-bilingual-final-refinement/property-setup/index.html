After the purchase

The sale may be complete, but the practical work often is not. Lianor is here to help.

Even a home sold as ready may still need internet, utility appointments, new keys, air conditioning, cleaning, furniture deliveries, and minor repairs before you can use it comfortably.

Lianor puts those tasks into a clear order, handles the agreed local follow-up, and tells you what is complete, what is waiting on a supplier, and what needs your decision.
